## Record During Work

- Log source rights, version, hash, licence decision, and citation anchor.
- Record model/provider snapshot and request configuration.
- Keep public, private, and sealed artifacts visibly separate.
- Explain non-obvious safety and abstention logic in comments.

## Verify Before Done

- Follow `TEST_CHECKLIST.md`.
- Read the complete diff.
- Update `HANDOVER.md` with state, evidence, blockers, and next action.
- Never use an AI summary as proof of rights, safety, or correctness.
